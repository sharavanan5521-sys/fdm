Who We Are

A Training & Consulting Partner Since 2000

Established in Kedah, Malaysia in 2000, Freedom Discovery Management is a professional training and Business Development Consulting partner, registered with HRD Corp. From the start we've empowered people and organisations through structured learning systems, strategic thinking frameworks and performance-driven consulting.

We're known for proprietary methodologies that blend neuroscience-based thinking models, coaching psychology and practical business strategy — designed to bridge capability gaps, lift productivity and produce measurable results.

Our mission is simple: to unlock human potential, strengthen leadership capability and drive sustainable business growth.

Meet the Founder

The Mind Behind Freedom Discovery

Coach Nan

Master Life Coach · ATD Master Trainer® · Leadership Strategist

Principal Consultant & Founder

Driven. Versatile. Transformative.

Coach Nan is an ATD Master Trainer® (ATD USA) and Certified Master Life Coach with over 28 years across consulting, business strategy, training and transformational coaching. He's known for practical, innovative solutions that drive both personal and organisational success.

He has personally trained more than 450,000 participants across Asia — from government bodies and MNCs to banks, manufacturers and educational institutions — and accumulated over 6,000 professional coaching hours guiding leaders, entrepreneurs and teams to break limitations and accelerate results.

A respected figure in the Malaysian coaching community, he served as elected Director of Membership for the Malaysian Association of Certified Coaches (MACC, 2020–2022), and brings 21+ years as a business strategist across Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia, India, Brunei and Cambodia. His mastery of NLP, hypnotherapy and emotional intelligence underpins the Pro Innothinking Mindset at the heart of Freedom Discovery.

Transformation begins from within. My mission is to equip every individual with the thinking tools, coaching skills and strategic clarity to achieve freedom — personally and professionally.

— Coach Nan
